Critical & High Priority Issues

Critical Schema
No schema.org markup on any page

Zero JSON-LD on Homepage, /en/ and /de/. AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini) are completely blind to peaceflow.ai as a named entity. SoftwareApplication, Organization, FAQPage — none of it present.

FixAdd JSON-LD with Organization + SoftwareApplication + FAQPage in <head> of every page. Minimum Viable Snippet: SoftwareApplication + Organization on the Homepage. Highest GEO uplift per development hour.
Critical Technical
robots.txt not present (HTTP 404)

robots.txt returns 404. AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended) receive no structured entry point and cannot find a sitemap.

FixCreate robots.txt in the repo root: User-agent: * Allow: / Sitemap: https://peaceflow.ai/sitemap.xml — explicitly allow all AI bots.
Critical Technical
sitemap.xml completely missing

/sitemap.xml returns 404. Crawlers must discover pages through link-following. The /de/ route is already 404 — without a sitemap, the German content is practically invisible to crawlers.

FixCreate sitemap.xml with all canonical URLs: /, /en/, /de/ (once fixed), legal pages — with lastmod, changefreq, and priority. Reference it in robots.txt.
Critical Technical
German route /de/ returns HTTP 404

https://peaceflow.ai/de/ returns 404. The primary German market URL is unreachable for both crawlers and users. hreflang tags on /en/ point to this 404 path.

FixCreate /de/index.html in the repo or set up a 301 redirect from /de → /. Update hreflang tags to use absolute URLs: https://peaceflow.ai/de/
Critical Technical
llms.txt not present

/llms.txt returns 404. For an AI product positioning itself as a smart companion, the absence of an AI-readable content description is a particularly embarrassing gap.

FixCreate /llms.txt: product description (2-3 sentences), key use cases, target audience, team, links to most important pages. Max 500 words. A one-time effort of 30 minutes.
Critical Technical
No canonical tags on any page

Neither Homepage nor /en/ nor /de/ have <link rel="canonical">. The Homepage is reachable under https://peaceflow.ai and possibly /index.html. Link equity fragments across URL variants.

FixAdd canonical tags to all pages: <link rel="canonical" href="https://peaceflow.ai/"> on Homepage, equivalent tags for /en/ and /de/.
Critical Brand
No Wikidata/Wikipedia entry — not in the Knowledge Graph

AICT Group and peaceflow.ai have no Wikipedia article, no Wikidata entry. For LLM retrieval pipelines, peaceflow.ai simply does not exist as an entity.

FixCreate a Wikidata entry for AICT Group / peaceflow.ai with sameAs links to domain, LinkedIn, and Crunchbase. This is the single GEO action with the highest leverage.
Critical Brand
No verified LinkedIn company profile found

No dedicated company profile found for peaceflow.ai or AICT Group on LinkedIn. LinkedIn company pages are a primary trust signal for AI systems.

FixCreate LinkedIn company pages for AICT Group (parent) and Peaceflow. Add logo, tagline, website link, 2-3 founders as employees, weekly posting cadence.
Critical E-E-A-T
No About/Team page — human expertise invisible

No /about, /team, or /founders page. The four managing directors (Alexander Lexa, Felix Degeler, Carlos Berná-Esteban, Patrik Garten) appear only in the legal imprint — no AI crawler evaluates this as an E-E-A-T signal.

FixCreate /en/about and /de/ueber-uns with team bios, photos, LinkedIn links, professional background, and a "why we built this" narrative. Add Person schema with sameAs.
Critical E-E-A-T
No credentials or methodology referenced

For a platform in conflict resolution and mental health (YMYL-adjacent), references to recognized frameworks (NVC, Harvard Negotiation Project, ICF, Austrian mediation law) are completely absent.

FixCreate a methodology page explaining which frameworks the AI is based on. List team certifications (CMC, ICF PCC, certified mediator) with issuing body.

FixExactly one H1 per page (primary topic). Demote all other current H1s to H2 or H3. Pure HTML change, no design impact.
