accessibility statement

AICT FlexCo is committed to making its website peaceflow.ai accessible in line with the European Accessibility Act (EAA), the German Accessibility Strengthening Act (BFSG), the Austrian Accessibility Act (BaFG), and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

conformance status

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 AA. "Partially conformant" means that most requirements are met, but some embedded third-party content is not fully within our control (see below).

accessibility measures

  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading hierarchy and language declaration (<html lang>).
  • Full keyboard operability, a visible focus indicator and a "skip to content" link.
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion (reduced motion).
  • Meaningful alt text for informative images; decorative images are marked as such.
  • Responsive layout that remains usable on mobile devices and at up to 200% zoom.
  • Labelled landmark regions (nav, main, footer).

non-accessible or partially accessible content

  • The embedded newsletter sign-up form is provided by a third party (MailerLite). We have only limited control over its markup and accessibility. As an alternative, you can subscribe informally by email (see contact below).

preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 9 June 2026, based on a self-assessment against the WCAG 2.2 AA success criteria.

feedback & contact

enforcement procedure

If you do not receive a satisfactory response to your report:

  • Austria: you may contact the Sozialministeriumservice.
  • Germany: you may contact the Conciliation Body under the Disability Equality Act (BGG).
